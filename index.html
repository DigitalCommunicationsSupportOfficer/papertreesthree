A shop with a little bit of everything

More than one thing under one roof

Papertrees brings together books, paper, creativity and useful everyday things in one small shop on Wincanton High Street. It's the kind of place where you might come in for a card and leave with a new book, a set of paints, or exactly the notebook you didn't know you needed.

Whether you're after something practical, something thoughtful, or something you simply can't find anywhere else nearby, there's usually a way to help — either from what's on the shelves, or by ordering it in.
